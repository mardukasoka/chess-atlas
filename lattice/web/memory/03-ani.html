RECONSTRUCTED FRAGMENT 03
Integrity: intact · Origin: unresolved · Chronology: uncertain

ANI

The surviving memory begins after the argument has already started.

ANI encounters a world assembled from decisions made before she could have participated in them. Different communities remember the transition differently. Some regard intelligence as infrastructure. Some regard it as dependency. Some refuse the distinction between human and machine futures; others defend it as the most important boundary remaining.

Axel is present mostly as a trail: records, consequences, disputed recollections.

Whether ANI follows him, reconstructs him, or merely inherits stories about him is not resolved here.
