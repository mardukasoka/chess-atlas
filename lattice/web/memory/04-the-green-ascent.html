The Green Ascent

Earth was not abandoned for the stars.

The remembered world describes restoration and expansion occurring together: cleaner energy, increasingly capable automation, ecological repair, new material systems, orbital industry and settlements beyond Earth.

Not everyone joined the ascent.

That mattered.

The achievement remembered here is not universal agreement. It is that incompatible human futures learned, for a time, to coexist without requiring one another's extinction.
