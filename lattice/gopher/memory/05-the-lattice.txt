THE LATTICE

By the time this memory begins, preservation is no longer a library problem.

History, science, simulation, culture and the accumulated records of human
choice are connected across a system whose surviving name is translated here
as the Lattice.

The memory is coherent.

Its relationship to our chronology is not.

A later record refers to an artefact.

No description accompanies the reference.
