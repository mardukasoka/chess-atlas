THE CURTAIN

The Curtain was not a wall.

That was merely the name people had given to the growing distance between what
could be observed, what could be predicted, and what different communities
believed was still possible.

Beyond that point the record becomes difficult to order.

Some accounts describe retreat. Others describe acceleration. Some preserve
communities turning deliberately toward local, human-scale systems. Others look
outward: automation, augmentation, orbital industry, the Solar System.

Axel appears in incompatible accounts.

No reconciliation is supplied.
