♚ king · ○ defenders · ● attackers · select a piece, then a marked destination

How to playAttackers move first. All pieces slide any distance orthogonally without jumping. Capture an ordinary piece by sandwiching it; restricted squares can complete a capture. Defenders win by moving the king to a corner. Attackers win by surrounding and capturing the king, or by completely encircling the defending force.StoryHnefatafl belongs to the asymmetric tafl games played across the Viking and medieval Norse world. No complete medieval rules text survives, so this board uses the Fetlar Hnefatafl Panel's 2007 reconstruction rather than presenting modern details as certain Viking rules.
