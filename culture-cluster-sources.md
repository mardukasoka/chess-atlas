# Culture-map source queue

Research notes for the next Atlas deep-time slice. These are source pointers only; map geometry remains schematic evidence envelopes rather than territorial borders.

- Çatalhöyük — UNESCO World Heritage Centre: Neolithic occupation c. 7400–6200 BCE; Chalcolithic west mound c. 6200–5200 BCE. https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1405
- Linear Pottery / LBK — archaeological literature places its emergence around 5550 cal BCE and spread across temperate Europe to about 4950 cal BCE. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0278416515000811
- Cucuteni–Trypillia — recent interdisciplinary work describes early phases from c. 4800 BCE, middle aggregation/megasites through the fourth millennium BCE, with late phases extending toward c. 3000 BCE. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11633957/
- Corded Ware — burials/material traits broadly c. 2800–2200 BCE across central, northern and eastern Europe. https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0155083

The Atlas must keep archaeological cultures distinct from inferred language, ancestry, ethnicity, statehood, or political control.
