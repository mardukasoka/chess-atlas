Alquerque de Doze

Playable Alfonso X, 1283 profile from the Libro de los Juegos. Later huffing, compulsory-capture and multi-jump conventions are kept separate rather than projected back into the medieval source.

● Black · ○ White · select a piece, then a marked destination

Secure historical core: 5×5 line board, twelve men per side, adjacent movement along drawn lines, capture by hopping an opponent, and victory by taking all opposing men.
