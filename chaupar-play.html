Chaupar / Chausar

A playable documented profile using three long dice. Chaupar rules vary across sources and traditions; this page does not present the profile as a universal ancient rule set.

Rules profile Typical documented Chaupar/Chausar profile: three long dice with values 1, 2, 5 and 6; fixed starts; split throws; no Pachisi castles or grace throws; same-colour pieces may form conglomerates; exact home and partnership finishing order apply. Captured pieces return to the Charkoni and re-enter on a later throw.
