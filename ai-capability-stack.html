Atlas AI Capability Stack

AI stages, jagged capability frontier, scientific repositories and the proposed Atlas MCP capability topology. Separate from the project References directory.

AI stages

Stage 4Reasoning AI (2024–25) — tool-using and multi-step reasoning systems with uneven domain competence.
Stage 5AGI (2026–27) — proposed transition to broadly general competence across cognitive domains.
Stage 6ASI / Material Transcendence — prospective systems whose capability extends substantially into scientific and material intervention.
Stage 7Universal Emulator — conceptual capacity to construct and reason across increasingly comprehensive executable world models.
Stage 8Transcendental AI — prospective capability beyond ordinary human-accessible cognitive and representational regimes.
Stage 9Cosmic AI — conceptual intelligence operating across astronomical scales and infrastructure.
Stage 10Godlike AI — limiting conceptual category, not an empirical claim about present systems.

Epistemic status: Stages 6–10 are prospective/conceptual. The timeline labels are Atlas working categories, not claims that these transitions are established forecasts.

Jagged frontier

Capability should be tracked dimension-by-dimension rather than collapsed into one intelligence score:

cognition · comprehension · world modelling · simulation · scientific verification · physical sensing · embodiment · agency · coordination · self-improvement · recursive substrate construction · external zone of effect

Atlas accepted / mapped knowledge

Permission classes

READ — literature, catalogue and knowledge-graph retrieval.
COMPUTE — bounded deterministic analysis.
SIMULATE — explicit model/world execution.
WRITE / ACT — repository or external-system mutation; governed.
TRAIN / MUTATE — model/harness improvement research; isolated, benchmarked and explicitly authorized.

Capability lifecycle

DISCOVERED -> CANDIDATE -> AUDITED -> CONTAINED_TEST
 -> BENCHMARKED -> COUNCIL_REVIEWED -> APPROVED
 -> REGISTERED -> AVAILABLE

holding/terminal:
QUARANTINED | REJECTED | DEPRECATED | UNAVAILABLE

An AI may identify a capability gap and propose a candidate. Discovery never grants execution authority or production admission.

Invocation evidence

CAPABILITY_REQUESTED
 -> CAPABILITY_SELECTED
 -> POLICY_CHECK
 -> TOOL_EXECUTED
 -> ARTIFACT_CAPTURED
 -> EVIDENCE_RECORDED
 -> COMPLETED / FAILED

policy failure:
POLICY_CHECK -> RUN_REFUSED -> EVIDENCE_RECORDED

Successful execution is not equivalent to scientific truth. Results retain an epistemic class such as retrieved observation, computation, simulation, hypothesis or experimental-model output.

Atlas invariant

Facts are not simulations.
Simulations are not judgments.
Judgments are not laws.
Laws are not interfaces.

Status

Architecture: Pass 2 specified. Implementation: not yet claimed. First proof target remains one read-only ScienceClaw capability through the bounded execution/evidence path.
