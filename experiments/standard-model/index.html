Quarks

u +⅔d −⅓c +⅔s −⅓t +⅔b −⅓

Spin ½ · colour charge · strong, weak and (when charged) electromagnetic interactions. Quarks feed directly into QCD bound states.

Leptons

e⁻νeμ⁻νμτ⁻ντ

Spin ½. Charged leptons participate in electromagnetic and weak interactions; neutrinos are neutral and weakly interacting.

Gauge bosons

γ photong gluons ×8W±Z⁰

Mediators of electromagnetic, strong and weak interactions.

Higgs

H⁰ · spin 0

Excitation of the Higgs field; part of electroweak symmetry breaking.
