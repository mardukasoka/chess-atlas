Interference & coherence

Interference follows probability amplitudes and phase coherence; it does not alone imply superfluidity or entanglement.

Entanglement & correlations

Bell testsphoton pairshigher-order correlations

Nonclassical correlations are distinct from optical bound states and collective phases.

Slow and anomalous light

EITdark-state polaritonsgroup velocity

Pulse delay or advancement depends on dispersion and reshaping. Negative group velocity does not allow faster-than-light signaling.

Photonic bound states

Rydberg mediumthree-photon bound statesoptical BIC

Photon–photon interactions mediated by matter can support few-body bound states. Optical bound states in the continuum arise by distinct confinement mechanisms.

Fluids of light

exciton–polaritonssuperfluid flowsupersolid order

Collective phases require additional evidence, such as coherent order and hydrodynamic behavior; they do not follow from photon entanglement alone.
