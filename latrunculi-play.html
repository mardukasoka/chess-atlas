Ludus Latrunculorum

Playable Ulrich Schädler 1994 reconstruction. The complete Roman rules do not survive, so this is an attributed modern reconstruction rather than a claim of a single canonical ancient ruleset.

Profile: alternating placement, orthogonal movement, leaps over occupied pieces, custodial immobilisation, delayed capture, and defeat when reduced to one piece. Board dimensions are a reconstruction choice; archaeological boards vary.
